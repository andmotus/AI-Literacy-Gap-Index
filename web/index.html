AI Literacy Gap Index

Region details

Click on a NUTS-1 region to inspect its index score, rank, cluster profile, and pillar values.

How to read the map

Darker regions indicate higher structural AI-literacy-gap risk. Grey regions are official NUTS-1 regions without sufficient index data.

The index does not directly measure individual AI literacy. It combines regional proxy indicators related to digital skills, social vulnerability, education, lifelong learning, labour-market exclusion, and ageing.
